# Background asset

`forest-bg.png` — 1086 × 1448 px (3:4 portrait), generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for this project. No external image API, paid asset purchase, or API key was used.

## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: static scenic backdrop for an endless bamboo climbing mobile browser game, portrait 3:4 illustration.
Primary request: a beautiful atmospheric Japanese bamboo forest landscape with layered sage green mountain hills, soft mist, and a warm cream sky with a distant small pale sun.
Style/medium: polished hand painted gouache illustration, subtle paper grain, restrained cinematic indie outdoor adventure game art, charming yet calm.
Composition/framing: portrait 3:4, distant bamboo grove silhouettes across the lower third and far sides, overlapping mountain layers in the middle and lower half, generous very pale warm open sky across the top half. Main subject is the landscape. Keep central foreground completely open so a separate 3D bamboo and climber can be overlaid. Trees are distant background silhouettes only.
Lighting/mood: gentle early morning daylight, atmospheric depth, fresh quiet mountain air, soft diffuse light.
Color palette: muted forest greens, sage, olive, teal distance, warm ivory sky, subtle warm sun.
Constraints: no foreground bamboo trunk, no central pole, no person, no characters, no buildings, no lettering, no text, no UI, no watermark. Opaque backdrop. Full bleed edge to edge.
